# The research is for reading, not for building

Owner ruling, 2026-08-09, standing. It governs every report that comes back from
the broadcast-systems research and everything anyone is tempted to do with one.

## The ruling, in the owner's own terms

Israel is the only market that matters right now. The international research
exists to find DOCUMENTATION of how these systems work, and then to ask one
question of each finding: is this relevant to Israel?

- If it is not relevant to Israel, it may be DOCUMENTED. It must not be built,
  and nothing in the product changes because of it.
- If it is relevant to Israel, it is a finding worth acting on.

## Why the research is worth running at all under that rule

Because of one specific case, which is the whole value of reading foreign
documentation: a foreign system's documentation names a term or a mechanism that
IS used in Israel, and we simply missed it, and there is no Israeli page on the
open web that mentions it. The trade here is small and largely undocumented
online, so an international manual can be the only written source for something
an Israeli traffic operator does every day.

So the research is a search for VOCABULARY AND MECHANISM WE ALREADY HAVE AND
CANNOT NAME, not a catalogue of features to import.

## What this forbids, concretely

- No new field, screen, rule, or engine behaviour whose only justification is
  "BARB does it" or "WideOrbit has it".
- No renaming of anything in the product to an international term that the
  Israeli trade does not use.
- No backlog item that reads "add X, seen in Y's documentation".

## What it permits

- A documentation file recording what a foreign system does and how, marked
  clearly as NOT APPLIED.
- A finding that says: this mechanism appears in foreign documentation, and here
  is the evidence that Israel does the same thing under a different name or
  under no name at all. That is actionable, and the evidence for the Israeli half
  has to be real, not inferred from the foreign half.

## The test each finding must pass before it becomes work

1. Is there evidence this happens in ISRAEL? Name it: the trade document, the
   owner's own words, the traffic file, the regulator, an Israeli source.
2. If the only evidence is foreign, the finding is documentation and stops there.
3. `docs/media-domain-from-the-trade.md` outranks every foreign manual on every
   question where the two disagree. It is a record of how this market actually
   works, from someone who works in it.

## Where the reports land

Foreign-only material goes to `docs/research/` and says on its first line that
it is not applied. Anything that clears the test above is raised as a finding
against the trade document, with its Israeli evidence attached.
