# Selling against an audience goal: what Israeli primary sources already say

Research record, 2026-08-09. Unlike the other files in this directory this one is
mostly PART ONE, relevant to Israel with Israeli evidence, because the sources
that answered are Israeli: the regulator's own rules and the buyers' own trade
guide.

`docs/media-domain-from-the-trade.md` outranks everything here. Where this file
adds anything, it is corroboration of that document by an independent primary
source, or a term the market uses that we did not have.

---

## 1. The billing basis is externally evidenced now, and it settles an ambiguity

The Israeli Marketing Association, a member of the rating committee, publishes in
its Media Guide at https://www.ishivuk.co.il/מדריך-מדיה-2/ :

> „שיטת התמחור המקובלת כיום היא לפי **עלות לנקודת רייטינג בתי אב יהודיים** לכל
> רצועת שידור… כולל **צפייה נדחית של 24 שעות מרגע השידור (overnight +1)**…
> התשלום בפועל מתבצע על פי CPP שנקבע כפול אחוז **רייטינג רבעי שעה** לכל ספוט
> כפול מקדם האורך."

All three terms the trade document names, in one sentence, plus the length
factor. It also gives the worked example: a spot at 8:03 bills on the 8:00 to
8:14 average.

**This overturns a correction made earlier the same day.** A search of the
measurement body's own publications had found only OVERNIGHT and no plus-one
form, and reported it NOT CONFIRMED. It is confirmed, and the reading is settled:
"+1" is a further 24 hours of deferred viewing from broadcast, not the following
morning's publication. See `israeli-rating-currency.md`, which keeps both.

Caveat: the guide is bylined 2014 and describes the pre-2017 channel structure.
No newer public Israeli source restates or contradicts the mechanics.

## 2. The market states our optimisation thesis as received wisdom

Same source:

> „כיוון שהתשלום עבור הספוט מתבצע לפי רייטינג רבעי שעה, וכיוון שבמהלך מקבצי
> פרסומות יורד הרייטינג ביחס לתכנית עצמה, **השאיפה היא למקסם את רייטינג הדקות
> ביחס לרייטינג רבעי השעות. נתון זה מושפע רבות ממיקום הספוט בתוך מקבץ הפרסומות.**"

Maximise the minute rating against the quarter-hour rating, driven by position
inside the pod. We are not inventing a lever; the market names it.

## 3. THE FINDING OF THE WAVE: Israeli law vests placement in the broadcaster

כללי מועצת תאגיד השידור הישראלי (שידור פרסומות…), תשפ״א-2021, clause 4(ב):

> „**שיבוץ מקבץ פרסום בלוח השידורים ושיבוץ תשדיר פרסום במקבץ פרסום ייעשו לפי
> שיקול דעתו הבלעדי של התאגיד, או מי מטעמו, בלי שהמזמין יהיה מעורב בהחלטה זו.**"

Placement of the pod in the schedule, AND of the spot within the pod, at the
broadcaster's sole discretion, with the orderer expressly not involved.
„המזמין" is legally defined as the media company, agency or advertiser.

The goal-based order's central premise, that the channel owns placement, is not a
commercial novelty an agency has to be talked into. For the public broadcaster it
is the codified default. That is a sales argument as much as an engineering one.

**Note the scope honestly: this is the public broadcaster's regime, not the
commercial one our operator sits in.** It establishes that the principle is
lawful and normal in this market. It does not establish it as our operator's
contractual position, which is a question for the owner.

## 4. A named Israeli channel already sells a GRP commitment

Same guide, on Channel 24:

> „לערוץ 24 מוקצה **תקציב מוגדר, תמורתו ניתנת התחייבות להשגת GRP** בהתאם ל-CPP
> שנקבע."

Budget in, GRP out, channel accountable. A real audience-goal sale documented in
Israel. Stated for that channel only; no equivalent was found for the two large
commercial channels, and its absence is not evidence either way.

## 5. A regulatory anchor for valuing a make-good in airtime

כללי הרשות השניה (שיבוץ תשדירי פרסומת), תשנ״ב-1992, clause 26,
„החזרת זמן פרסומת שלא נוצל":

> „(1) בעד כל דקת פרסומת שלא שודרה… בין השעות **20:00 ו־24:00 (שעת שיא)** –
> **דקה בשעת שיא או שלוש דקות בשעה שאינה שעת שיא**;
> (2) בעד כל דקת פרסומת שלא שודרה בשעה שאינה שעת שיא – דקה בשעה כאמור או
> **שליש דקה בשעת שיא**;"

**Israeli law itself sets one prime minute equal to three non-prime minutes**,
symmetric in both directions, and independently fixes prime as 20:00 to 24:00.

Read it for what it is: a REGULATOR-SIDE restitution mechanism, compensating the
licensee for inventory lost to a news pre-emption, requiring the Director's prior
written approval. It is not the commercial make-good an advertiser receives. What
it gives us is a defensible published daypart substitution rate, where the
alternative was inventing one.

## 6. The market's own name for the lever we built

The guide defines GRP as a households base and TRP as a target-audience base, and
then names the ratio:

> „**יחס המרה** מבטא את היחס שבין רייטינג קהל המטרה לרייטינג בתי אב (**TRP חלקי
> GRP**)… מכיוון ש**מרבית הקמפיינים נרכשים על בסיס CPP לבתי אב**, מיקסום ה-TRP
> באותו תקציב נתון מביא להוזלת עלות ההגעה לקהל היעד."

**Campaigns are PLANNED against a target-audience goal and BOUGHT AND PAID on a
household CPP.** The gap between the two is the trade's own named efficiency
measure, and it is exactly the divergence the goal-based order design calls the
whole mechanism. Israeli buyers already optimise it by hand.

## 7. Competitive separation is commercial, not regulatory: a negative read

The 1992 rules were read specifically for a competing-advertiser separation rule
and for a position rule. **Neither exists.** The only adjacent clause is a
three-second transition marker, which is a presentation rule.

This confirms the trade document, which lists competitive separation among the
run PRIORITIES while keeping regulatory checks as a separate switch. The document
was right and the regulator's own text now backs it.

## 8. A tension worth putting back to the owner

The public guide names preferred positions as „ראשון, שני, שלישי ו**אחרון**" with
a premium of 5 to 20 percent, names „תשדירי T&T" as an Israeli product priced on
combined length, and gives the gold break „תוספת של עד 25%".

The trade document says the product must model **1 to 5 plus L**. The owner
confirmed on 2026-08-09 that `L` is what the market uses.

So a current first-hand source and a 2014 public guide differ on how far the
ordinals run. The trade document outranks the guide and the product is not
changing. It is recorded because it is the one place a primary source and the
trade document differ on a number, and the owner can settle it in a sentence.

## 9. The trap: our make-good ledger is LARGER than the foreign one

Foreign primary sources, from company filings rather than marketing, define
compensation for an audience shortfall as an **audience deficiency unit**: paid
in airtime rather than cash, carried as a liability, and arising "as a matter of
industry practice rather than as a matter of written contract". They distinguish
it from a make-good, which cures a CARRIAGE failure rather than an AUDIENCE one.

Useful vocabulary. But the deployed foreign systems bind the cure to the
deficient deal, with no cross-deal credit, while the trade document describes an
Israeli ledger held at three levels where an agency accrues credit and may spend
it on a DIFFERENT campaign later, taken as added media rather than cash.

**Israel's object is strictly larger.** Anyone importing the tidier foreign
formulation because it is well documented would silently ship the smaller thing.
The vocabulary transfers; the model does not.

Also recorded: no Hebrew trade term for make-good was found publicly, across five
phrasings. Only the regulatory restitution above and commercial „בונוסים".

## 10. What could not be established

- Which traffic or sales system any Israeli channel actually runs today. No
  source, in either language.
- The incumbent named in the trade document has ZERO public web presence. Two
  independent research streams reached that separately. The absence is the
  result, and it is why the trade document is the only written source.
- No formal audience guarantee documented for the two large commercial channels.
- No published Israeli rate card exists; an Israeli agency states this outright.
- Whether the 1992 rules formally bind today's licensees, since they are drafted
  for franchisees. Inferred only, and not asserted.
