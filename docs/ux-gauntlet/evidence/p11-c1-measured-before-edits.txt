DEFECTS I MEASURED BEFORE TOUCHING ANYTHING
  1. The reference figure is not on the row. The row prints "4.4 of 35 rating points",
     "counted over 1 of 7 broadcast days" and "88%". 88% is 4.4 / 5.0 and 5.0 appears nowhere
     as a number; it is only the position of an unlabelled mark on the bar whose title gives
     the rule and not the value. A reader who divides what is on the screen gets 12.6%.
  2. A refused write says nothing. Submitting an offer whose window closes before it opens:
     the API answers 400 with a bilingual refusal, the form closes immediately and discards
     what was typed, and polling the whole document every 100 ms for 2.5 s finds no toast and
     no refusal text anywhere. Root cause outside this piece: workspace-router.jsx:152 renders
     CampaignsPage without a notify prop, so ClientsWorkspace falls back to its no-op default
     and every notice this piece emits at its published address is swallowed.
  3. A campaign name in the ledger is a dead end. Clicking "2025-04 - עוגן הלוואות חברתיות"
     returns to the board with focus on row 0, a different campaign; the wanted row is index 6
     of 56, unscrolled and unmarked.
  4. Bidi: in the English pass the campaign name element computes direction ltr, so a reader
     scanning left to right meets its three parts out of order. Logical
     "2025-04 - עמותת מל"י - מל"י"; measured paint positions x=359 for the period, x=430 for the
     trailing מל"י and x=502 for עמותת, so a left-to-right scan reads period, brand, advertiser.
     Stated precisely: the Hebrew run's internal layout was always right, and what was wrong was
     the element's own direction, which put the name's parts in front of the reader in an order
     the name does not have.
  5. The board's basis line prints the raw ISO instant 2025-04-27T22:59:00 while the ledger
     prints 2025-04-27 22:59 through the piece's own instant() helper.
  6. The day drill re-authors words campaigns_delivery.py already publishes: it says
     "Booked, not aired yet" / "מוזמן, טרם שודר" where AIR_STATE_VOCABULARY says
     "Scheduled, not aired yet" / "מתוזמן, טרם שודר".
  7. The ledger's empty state promises a control no row on this data carries: "A campaign with
     a measured shortfall carries the control that raises a make-good". Measured: 0 of 56 rows
     reach remedyFor kind raise, because every flight has unsourced remaining days.
  8. The surface and the API disagree about when a make-good may be raised. POST /api/make-goods
     on CMP_D040 returns 201 with deficit_kind to_date and deficit_value 0.6; the surface never
     offers that raise on any row.
  9. The offer input is labelled "Offer, in the shortfall unit" and never names the unit.
 10. Withdraw and revoke fire on one click with no confirm and no reason, against Stripe's
     required reason and "Yes, cancel refund" confirm.
