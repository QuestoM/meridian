# P12 contract: model improvement

The section 8.8 publication for P12: what it owns, what it wrote, the shapes it
emits, and the one thing it could not do and why. Every number below was taken
on the delivered tree, after the last edit, by `wc -l`, by `pytest`, by
`/usr/bin/time` or by running the command that produces it.

P12 owns `scripts/adopt_candidate.py`, `models/candidates/**` and
`models/releases/**` by handover from P7, plus the frontend tree
`tv-break-dashboard/src/model/candidates/**`. That tree was empty until round 5
and this sentence still said so through round 9; it now holds a built and
measured panel, and what remains is the two-line mount in section 6, which is
the lead's.

## 1. What was missing, measured

P7 built the model console and it answers "what does this candidate say". It
does not answer JS-19's actual question, which is "is this candidate genuinely
better than what is live". Three things were absent and all three are measured
absences rather than opinions:

- **No comparison of the predictors.** Every held-out figure the console shows
  is read out of the artifact's own metadata, written by its own fit under its
  own split against its own target. Comparing two of those compares two
  experiments. Nothing scored two artifacts on one set of breaks.
- **No adoption act.** `models/tv_break_coefficients.json` had never been
  written by anything but the training script. There was no backup, no undo, no
  record of who adopted what, and no verdict inside the artifact.
- **No check on what else an adoption would change.** Measured on this tree, the
  candidate that already carries a ship verdict drops `first_break_multiplier`,
  which `kairos/service.py:117` folds into the optimizer assumptions on every
  run. Nothing could see that.
- **Added in round 3. No comparison of the coefficients themselves.** JS-19's
  done condition names three things recorded against a new model version: the
  gate deltas, the **coefficient deltas** and the measured money movement. Two
  were built. The third was in no payload on this piece or on the console.
  Measured on this tree before round 3: the shipped artifact and the
  `afterwindow` candidate differ in **all 36 of 36** coefficients, the largest
  point move being 0.014066885 at `PrimeShow2_first_short`, and
  `artifact_surface()` emitted no key naming any of it. What the checks printed
  in its place was "credible bounds moved on 72 of the 72 bounds", which invites
  exactly the wrong reading: that the intervals moved and the points did not.
- **Added in round 6. The evaluation's own honesty sentence was a constant, and
  on this tree it is false.** Every payload carries one sentence that decides how
  every figure on it may be read: that each artifact was fitted on all of the
  breaks it is now scored on, so the in-sample optimism is common to every row
  and the difference between two rows survives it. It was a literal in
  `adopt_candidate_words.py`, asserted of every tree, checked against nothing.
  Measured: five artifacts record `total_breaks_measured` 2532 and `spotclip`
  records **2336**, its own metadata naming the cause as `base_breaks` 2532 and
  `dropped_by_spot_clip` **196**. All six are scored on the same 2,532. So 196 of
  the breaks `spotclip` is scored on were never in its fit and are in the fit of
  every row it is compared against, the optimism is not common-mode, and **the
  row this is true of is the row the table ranks first**. The figure that
  falsifies the sentence was already being computed: `breaks_fitted_on` was on
  every candidate row and in `holdout_rescores.json`, and nothing compared it to
  anything.
- **Added in round 6. The same row carries its own producer advising against
  it.** `spotclip.metadata.holdout_clean_test` records `adopt_recommended: false`
  with the reason `variant rmse improves base by -0.38% (threshold 2%): keep OFF
  (within noise)`, taken on 461 breaks. No payload on this piece carried it. It
  is not a figure this surface ranks, and it is not treated as one: a self-test
  is the artifact's own split under its own fit, which is the comparison this
  whole piece exists to replace, so it is carried per row, never sorted, and the
  sentence saying it is not comparable travels with it every time it prints.
- **Added in round 8. The gates were on no screen, and the count that stood in
  for them reads backwards.** JS-19's sequence reads the gates *before* it reads
  the money, and its target asks for every gate delta with its held-out figure.
  Measured before building: the board this piece publishes carried **no gate key
  at all**, and the only surface that had them was the `checks` command, which is
  the last command a steward runs. Worse, the reading they had was wrong.
  `model_console_candidates.gate_deltas` returns every gate key on which two
  artifacts do not hold the same value, and a key the candidate does not carry
  comes back as one of them. Measured: `afterwindow`, `competitor` and `spotclip`
  each return **ten** such rows and hold a different value on **none** of them,
  because they carry none of them; `placebo_corrected` returns two, both
  absences. Counting the rows reads as ten gates decided the other way when the
  truth is none. Across the whole shelf **exactly one** key on one candidate is a
  real disagreement: `placebo_correction_active`, `true` on the shipped artifact
  and `false` on `calibrated`.
- **Added in round 8. The held-out sentence was a constant and on one pair it is
  false.** The sentence that says two gate figures taken on different amounts are
  not comparable was asserted of every pair. Measured: on `calibrated` all three
  held-out blocks are reported by both artifacts at the same amount, so the
  surface was stating a confound that pair does not carry. On `competitor` it is
  real, and it is the argument for this whole piece: the same counter-programming
  gate was decided on **2,532** breaks by the shipped artifact and on **506** by
  the candidate. The rule and whether it bit are two sentences now, and the second
  is a measurement.
- **Added in round 10. What each artifact was for was on disk and on no screen.**
  Every candidate's metadata may carry a one-line `purpose`. Measured before this
  round: `afterwindow` records `after-window de-bias verification recompute
  (post-clip code, reference data)`, and that sentence appeared **zero** times in
  the published board, zero times in `show`, zero times in `checks` and zero
  times in `diff`. The word `purpose` appeared **once** on the whole surface, as
  one of forty-three metadata key NAMES in the list of keys an adoption would
  add. So a steward opened a shelf of five opaque identifiers, `spotclip`,
  `afterwindow`, `calibrated`, `competitor` and `placebo_corrected`, and inferred
  what each one was trying to do from its coefficient table. The reference puts
  this first: a run overview leads with the notes.
- **Added in round 10. No artifact said what data it read, and nothing checked
  it.** Every one of the six records `source_fingerprints`, three files with a
  digest each, and no surface named a file, compared a digest against the disk or
  compared two artifacts with each other. Measured now: all six record the same
  three files at the same digests, all three are on disk with those exact bytes,
  and those are the bytes the evaluation rebuilds its breaks from. So the source
  data is **not** one of the differences between any two rows, which is a
  measurement that strengthens the comparison rather than qualifying it, and it
  was not on the surface at all.
- **Added in round 2. No way to record the verdict from the surface that takes
  it.** JS-19 ends with "record the verdict" and this terminal had `show`,
  `rescore`, `measure`, `checks`, `adopt`, `revert` and `report` and no way to
  record one, so the steward crossed to another surface for the one act the
  story is named after. Worse, the verdict he took there could not rest on the
  common-basis comparison, because that comparison was on a file nobody reads.
  Measured on this tree: **all five verdicts on record predate it**, so every
  one was taken by reading two artifacts' own held-out figures, on different
  test sets.

## 3. Shapes frozen

**A cell delta**, emitted per candidate on every re-score row, and the shape
the whole of round 3 turns on.

```
{rows: [{cell, shipped, candidate, delta, state, breaks,
         squared_error_delta, share_of_absolute}],
 summary: {cells_compared, cells_moved, cells_unchanged,
           cells_added: [cell], cells_dropped: [cell],
           max_abs_delta, max_abs_delta_at, breaks_weighted_mean_abs_delta,
           net_squared_error_delta, total_abs_squared_error_delta,
           cancelled_share, cells_improved, cells_worsened,
           carries_the_move: [cell], carries_share, concentrated,
           reading_en, reading_he, key_shape_en, key_shape_he}}
```

`state` is `moved`, `unchanged`, `added` or `dropped`, and a cell only one
artifact carries has `delta: null`. It is never a zero, because a missing
coefficient is not a coefficient of zero.

`squared_error_delta` is the candidate's squared error minus the shipped
model's, summed over the breaks in that cell, so it is signed the way the metric
is signed and it sums over the cells to the whole movement the metric reports.
That is what makes each cell attributable rather than merely listed.

`cancelled_share` is the figure a metric delta cannot state. A candidate that
moves every cell and nets a thousandth is not a model that improved by a
thousandth; it is a re-fit that landed somewhere else. Measured on this tree,
`competitor` cancels 98.7 percent of its own movement, and its RMSE delta of
0.000011 is what is left over.

`concentrated` decides whether the cells carrying the move are named. It takes
16 of `competitor`'s 36 cells to reach four fifths of its movement, and a list of
16 names is the opposite of the finding, so the payload says so and the render
says the movement is spread.

**The board**, emitted by `adopt_candidate.py publish` into
`tv-break-dashboard/src/model/candidates/candidate-board.json`, which is the
file the panel imports.

```
{published_at, measured_at, fingerprint, rescore_state,
 evaluation, limit, baselines, cell_structure, structure_finding,
 duplicate_groups,
 shipped: {rmse, sha256, short, file, cells,
           version_id, version_name, version_short, computed_at},
 candidates: [{id, file, bytes, sha256, short, computed_at, breaks_fitted_on,
               rmse, rmse_delta, paired_statistic, paired_bar, fold_dispersion,
               breaks_improved, breaks_worsened,
               verdict, verdict_en, verdict_he, rule_en, rule_he,
               duplicate_of, adopted,
               money: {state, revenue_delta, last_known_revenue_delta,
                       measured_at, rows, basis, whole_plan_delta,
                       reason_en, reason_he},
               decision: {state, recorded_at, actor, count, on_rescore},
               cells: {compared, moved, added, dropped, max_abs_delta,
                       max_abs_delta_at, cancelled_share, concentrated,
                       carries_the_move, carries_share,
                       reading_en, reading_he, key_shape_en, key_shape_he,
                       top: [cell row], top_of}}]}
```

Three properties are load-bearing and each is a test. Every `sha256` is the
digest of the file the row was measured on, which is what makes the panel's
freshness tri-state possible against a route rather than a promise. `top` is the
ranked head of the cell rows and `top_of` says how many there were, so a screen
showing eight of thirty-six has said so. And the payload carries no command, no
free text from a decision record and no name that reaches the training act; the
write refuses if one appears, because the panel is behind a route wall and the
bundle it travels in is not.

```
 paired: {rmse_delta, mean_squared_error_delta, standard_error,
          paired_statistic, paired_bar, fold_rmse_deltas, fold_dispersion,
          folds, breaks_improved, breaks_worsened, breaks},
 verdict: {state: identical | better | worse | not_distinguishable,
           en, he, rule_en, rule_he, clears_paired_bar, clears_fold_dispersion}}
```

**The fit basis**, added in round 6, carried on the evaluation and per row.

```
{fit_basis: {state: common | uneven | unknown, scored_on,
             rows: [{id, fitted_on, base, dropped, scored_on, state,
                     not_fitted_on, share_not_fitted_on}],
             uneven: [id], unknown: [id],
             largest_shortfall, largest_shortfall_at, largest_shortfall_share}}
```

`state` on a row is `all`, `fewer` or `unknown`, and `unknown` is not a match:
an artifact that records nothing about its own fit has not agreed with the
others, nobody asked it. A row recording a fit over MORE breaks than the
evaluation scores is `all` with `not_fitted_on` 0, never a negative shortfall.

**The gate reading**, added in round 8, carried on every candidate row of the
registry, of the board and of a verdict record.

```
{gates: {state: same | absent_only_candidate | absent_only_mixed |
                decides_differently,
         not_identical, differing, absent, absent_on_shipped,
         reading_en, reading_he, differing_keys: [key],
         rows: [{key, shipped, candidate, shipped_text, candidate_text,
                 shipped_absent, candidate_absent}],
         held_out: [{block, shipped_size, shipped_unit, shipped_unit_en,
                     shipped_unit_he, candidate_size, candidate_unit,
                     candidate_unit_en, candidate_unit_he,
                     shipped_absent, candidate_absent, comparable}],
         held_out_blocks, held_out_comparable, held_out_uneven,
         held_out_one_sided, held_out_state: even | uneven | none,
         held_out_rule_en, held_out_rule_he,
         held_out_basis_en, held_out_basis_he}}
```

Four properties are load-bearing.

`not_identical` is every key the two artifacts do not agree on, which is what the
console's comparison returns, and `differing` is the subset where **both carry a
value and the values differ**. Only the second is a gate that decided
differently, and the sentence is chosen from the second.

**No count here is a share of all gate keys.** The list of keys the console
compares is a constant inside a frozen module, this piece reads the comparison
rather than the list, and a key both artifacts leave out is indistinguishable
from a key both agree on from the comparison alone. A test asserts that no
reading states such a share.

`shipped_text` and `candidate_text` are the rendered form of a stored value,
produced once on the side that still knows its type. A stored `1.0` prints as
`1.0` at this terminal and as `1` in a browser, because JavaScript has one number
type, and two surfaces of one piece printing one stored value two ways is a
divergence a steward walks into inside a session.

`held_out_rule_*` is unconditional and `held_out_basis_*` is a measurement about
this pair. The two used to be one sentence asserting unevenness of every pair,
which is false of `calibrated`.

**What the artifact was built for and what it read**, added in round 10, carried
on every re-score row, every registry row, every board row and every checks
payload.

```
{origin: {id, purpose, purpose_state: recorded | absent,
          purpose_reading_en, purpose_reading_he,
          sources: [{file, sha256, short, on_disk, matches}],
          sources_recorded, sources_state: verified | moved |
                                           unverifiable | absent,
          sources_changed: [file], sources_reading_en, sources_reading_he,
          same_sources_as_shipped: same | differs | unknown,   <- absent on the
          differs_on: [file],                                     shipped row
          agreement_reading_en, agreement_reading_he,
          recipe_state: not_recorded, recipe_en, recipe_he,
          recipe_unblocked_by_en, recipe_unblocked_by_he}}
```

Four properties are load-bearing.

`purpose` is the producer's own sentence, carried verbatim and never rewritten,
never summarised and never inferred. Two of the five candidates record none, and
that is `absent` with the field that would supply it, because everything needed
to write a plausible purpose for those two rows is already on the row and none
of it may become one.

`sources_state` is **the engine's own verdict, called rather than
reimplemented**. `kairos.model.freshness.coefficient_freshness` is the function
the product already uses to decide whether stored coefficients still match the
data on disk, so a source line here and the engine's staleness verdict cannot
disagree about one artifact. Its `unknown` covers two different facts and they
are separated here: `absent` is a producer that recorded nothing, `unverifiable`
is a tree that has lost an input, and the acts that fix them are different.

`matches` is tri-state per file. True, false, or null when the file is not on
disk, because a file nobody could hash did not fail the comparison.

`same_sources_as_shipped` is the confound the fit-basis count cannot see: two
artifacts fitted over the same NUMBER of breaks out of two different source
files are not comparable at all. The keys are absent rather than `same` on the
shipped artifact's own row, because a row agreeing with itself is a true
sentence about nothing.

**What the artifact's own producer recorded**, added in round 6, per row.

```
{self_reported: {id, state: recommended | advised_against |
                        recorded_without_a_verdict | absent,
                 n_test, reason, adopt_recommended, reading_en, reading_he}}
```

Never sorted and never a column. `adopt_recommended` is tri-state: `true`,
`false`, or `null` when a self-test exists and reaches no recommendation, which
is `afterwindow` on this tree. Folding that into either answer would put a
verdict in the artifact's mouth that it never gave.

**The evaluation block**, which every payload carries.

```
{evaluation: {breaks, cells, window_from, window_to,
              target_en, target_he, metric_en, metric_he,
              target_sd, target_sd_en, target_sd_he, folds},
 limit: {state: in_sample | in_sample_uneven | in_sample_unknown,
         en, he, unblocked_by_en, unblocked_by_he,
         uneven: [id], unknown: [id],
         largest_shortfall, largest_shortfall_at, largest_shortfall_share},
 cell_structure: {cell_mean_loo_rmse, global_mean_loo_rmse, rmse_delta,
                  earns_its_place, out_of_sample, reading_en, reading_he},
 fingerprint, inputs: {shipped, candidate:<id>, data:<file>}}
```

Round 8 replaced `window`, which was emitted pre-joined as
`2024-11-01 to 2024-11-30`, with its two ends. An English preposition inside a
machine date was being rendered verbatim into a Hebrew line, and nothing on the
reading side could take it apart, because splitting a joined date at a call site
is exactly the arithmetic `verify-date-rules.mjs` exists to ban. The joining now
belongs to whoever reads it: this terminal joins in English through
`words.window_line`, and the board hands both ends to `shell/dates.js`
`formatSpan`, which is the one file in the dashboard that decides what a date
looks like. Measured on the real screen in both locales: `01/11/2024-30/11/2024`,
and no `2024-11-01` anywhere on it.

Round 2 added the second half of five display fields that shipped in English
only: `rule_he` on every verdict, `metric_he` in the evaluation, `basis_he` on
both baselines, `how_he` on every check and `he` on every next act. All of them
live in one table in `adopt_candidate_words.py` and a test walks that table, so
a key added without its partner fails rather than shipping half translated.
`target_sd` was computed on every row and rendered nowhere; it is one figure per
evaluation now, because it is a property of the target and not of a row, and it
is on screen because an rmse of 0.241161 against a target spread of 0.241379 is
the most honest line this surface carries.

## 4. The rules this piece holds

**The training line.** Adoption writes `models/`, so by section 4.1 it is
training, so it is company staff only and unreachable. Five tests hold it: every
file a full adopt and revert cycle creates is measured and asserted to be under
`models/`, every file the verdict act creates is measured the same way and lands
under `models/releases/`, no module in `kairos_api/` mentions it, no file the
operator's interface ships mentions it, and no route the running application
publishes is handled out of `scripts/`. Re-measured in round 10 on a server
started for it on port 8043: the running application publishes **159 paths and
none of them matches `adopt`**, and the operator's interface and `kairos_api/`
together mention this act **zero** times. The paths added since round 3 are
other wave-two pieces, not this one.

**Deciding and adopting are two acts and the line between them is the same
line.** Recording a verdict writes `models/releases/decisions.jsonl`, so it is
training by the same test and it is offered on the same terminal and nowhere
else. It moves no artifact, and the store's own `adoption` block says so inside
every ship record it writes.

**The escalation.** An adoption whose measured movement is not zero stops and
will not land, and no command-line flag releases it. What releases it is
`models/releases/owner_approvals/<candidate>.json` naming the exact movement in
shekels; an approval that names a different figure does not release it, and an
approval goes out of date by itself when the measured figure moves. Three tests
cover the three branches.

**Reversibility.** The replaced artifact is copied whole before anything is
written. A revert restores those bytes exactly, refuses when the shipped
artifact is not the one that adoption left behind, and refuses a second time.

**Honest math.** The evaluation states its own limit on every payload: all six
artifacts were fitted on all 2,532 breaks and no unseen data exists in this
repository, so every absolute figure is optimistic and only the paired
difference is readable. The two baselines are genuinely out of sample and are
labelled as the only figures that are.

**Tri-state on the coefficient delta.** A cell both artifacts carry has a delta.
A cell only one carries has `null` and the word `added` or `dropped`. A
candidate that moves nothing has no cancellation figure and the column reads
`no move`, because printing `0.0 percent` there would state that its movement
cancelled rather than that there was none.

**The competitor boundary, measured on the keys rather than asserted.** The cell
keys look like `PrimeShow2_first_short` and a reader who has not been told will
read the first part as a channel. A test walks every key in the shipped artifact
and asserts each is one of the four programme classes, one of three break
positions and one of three break lengths, so a key shaped like anything else
fails rather than shipping. The sentence saying so travels with the figures, in
both languages, on the terminal and inside every payload.

  beside them: a movement against that row's own fold dispersion, a cell's share
  of the whole absolute movement, and the live model's error against the spread
  of the effect it predicts. That last one fills to **99.91 percent**, which is
  the finding the numerals could not show. A browser test reads each bar's drawn
  width out of the real layout and compares it with the share the payload
  carries, because a bar that draws a shape nobody measured is the visual form of
  a fabricated figure and would pass every other test in the file.

  **One rule this round used that is not written down anywhere.** A data bar is
  not the banned one-sided accent bar of design rule 1 and it is not any of the
  other seven rules; `design-rules.md` has no entry for a meter, a share bar or a
  chart mark, and section 8 of that file says to add the rule before applying it.
  That file is not on this piece's ownership row, so the rule is reported here
  rather than written there. What this row does, and what an entry would say: a
  bar is always a share of a denominator that is on the screen in words, it is
  sized by `inline-size` alone so it mirrors with the document, it spends no
  state colour on its track, and it is hidden from assistive technology because
  the figure it draws is always beside it in text.
- **It did not re-run any training recipe.** Re-fitting each candidate under its
  own flags on a training split would be the clean out-of-sample comparison, and
  it is impossible here for two reasons that are both measured: three of the five
  candidates were produced by scripts other than the training script and carry
  none of its gate metadata, and two of them redefine the measurement window, so
  they do not agree with the shipped artifact on what the target is. The
  evaluation says so in words rather than averaging over the disagreement.
